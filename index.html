More Than a Café, a Place to Call Home

Sonny Ray is a neighbourhood café where the team knows the regulars by name and the service is as warm as the sun.

Our space, located on the corner of Auburn Road, is a peaceful haven designed to be enjoyed. With a main dining room of white walls and wooden furniture, a cozy plant-filled backyard and a dog-friendly terrace, every corner is designed to make you feel at home.

Whether it's a quiet breakfast, brunch with friends, or a break for the best coffee in Melbourne, you'll find the perfect atmosphere at Sonny Ray.
